Getting Started

  1. Open chatgpt.com in a tab and make sure you're logged in.
  2. Click the Anthill Spider extension icon in your toolbar.

Find All Conversations

  • Click Find All Conversations to auto-scroll through your entire sidebar and count how many conversations you have.
  • This doesn't scrape anything yet — it just discovers what's there.
  • ChatGPT lazy-loads sidebar items, so this may take a minute if you have hundreds of conversations.

Batch Export

  1. Set the Conversations field to the number of most-recent conversations you want (or leave at 0 for all).
  2. Click Start Export. Confirm the prompt.
  3. Each conversation opens in a background tab, is scraped, and the tab closes. Don't close Chrome during this.
  4. You can Pause at any time and Resume later — even after restarting Chrome.
  5. If you Stop early, all progress is saved and downloaded automatically.
  6. Save Progress Now downloads what's been scraped so far without stopping.

Single Conversation Export

  1. Click Pick a Conversation.
  2. A crosshair overlay appears on the ChatGPT page.
  3. Click any conversation in the sidebar to scrape and download just that one.
  4. Press Esc to cancel pick mode.

Output Format

  • Exports are JSON files containing conversation metadata and all messages with roles (user/assistant).
  • Partial exports include a "partial": true flag in metadata.
  • Filenames show progress: e.g. chatgpt_conversations_47of120_partial.json

Interrupted Sessions

  • If Chrome crashes or the extension restarts during a scrape, your progress is saved automatically.
  • Next time you open the popup, you'll see a Resume banner with options to continue, discard, or download what was already scraped.
